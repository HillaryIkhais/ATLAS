# Proof: This Isn't Theoretical

We attacked ATLAS eight ways. Over-limited requests were blocked. Expired capabilities were rejected. Revoked states were prevented. Wrong agents, replay attempts, wrong policies, fake evidence, and wrong sources—all reverted.

These weren't edge cases imagined in isolation. They were executed against live contracts on Sepolia and Creditcoin testnets, with real USC proofs and cryptographic verification. The failures are reproducible, the guards are enforceable, and the invariant—capability.scope ≤ policy(scope)—holds across every attack vector.

This isn't a theory of mind. It's what happens when you give an agent bounded authority derived from verified reality and someone tries to exceed it.
